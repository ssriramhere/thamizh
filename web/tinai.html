Five landscapes, five moods of love

In the akam poems the landscape tells you the feeling before a word of it is said. Pick a tiṇai to see its time, its season, its deity, its flower and the poems set there.

Landscapes, moods, times, seasons and deities as the Tolkāppiyam sets them out in its book on subject matter (Poruḷatikāram). The Tolkāppiyam gives pālai no land or deity of its own; for pālai these follow the later tradition.
