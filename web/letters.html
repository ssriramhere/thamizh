Consonants

Each consonant first with a short a (க, ka), then pure, with the dot (க், the mey). A pure consonant can't be said alone, so it is named with a short i before it, as in "ik". Every sound comes with a word to hear it in.

Consonant + vowel

Join a consonant to a vowel and you get one of 216 letters (uyirmey). Tap any letter to hear it and see how it is built. A long vowel is held twice as long as a short one: two mātirai against one.
