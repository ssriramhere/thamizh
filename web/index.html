The landscape tells you the mood

Every Caṅkam love poem is set in one of five landscapes, its tiṇai. Mountains mean union; the seashore means pining. The landscapes arrive with the Caṅkam poems.

How the readings are made

Text

Five open digital editions, compared couplet by couplet. Where they disagree, the reading that scans wins.

Meter

A rule engine built on Tolkāppiyam and Yāpparuṅkalakkārikai. No AI decides the meter.

Translations

Generated by AI from the Tamil text, the word splits and the scansion, and labelled as such.
